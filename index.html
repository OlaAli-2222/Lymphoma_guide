NEWLY DIAGNOSED?

Start with three questions.

A diagnosis can bring unfamiliar words and decisions. It is okay to ask your care team to explain things more than once.

Open appointment checklist →
1
What is my exact lymphoma subtype?

Ask for the diagnosis as written in your pathology report.

2
What tests do I need next?

Ask how each test helps clarify the diagnosis or stage.

3
Why is this plan recommended for me?

Ask how the plan fits your subtype, stage, health, and preferences.
