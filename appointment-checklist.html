Before you leave the appointment

  • Do you know the next step and who is responsible for arranging it?
  • Do you know when and how results will be shared?
  • Do you have contact details for questions and urgent symptoms?
  • Do you understand what to do if you do not hear back by the expected date?

Bring if available

  • Pathology report
  • Recent test results
  • Medication list
  • Questions and notes

Remember

It is okay to ask the team to explain unfamiliar terms, write down a plan, or repeat information.
