Privacy & medical disclaimer

Important information about what this educational website can and cannot do.

Educational information only

Lymphoma Guide is an educational project for patients, families, and caregivers. It is not a medical provider and does not provide diagnosis, individualized medical advice, treatment recommendations, or emergency care.

Do not delay medical care

Do not start, stop, or change treatment based on this website. Discuss your diagnosis, test results, medicines, symptoms, and treatment choices with a qualified healthcare professional who knows your situation. For severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, seek urgent medical help.

External resources

This website links to third-party organizations for convenience. We do not control their content, privacy practices, services, or availability. Check the current information on each organization’s own website.

Privacy in this version

This static version does not include accounts, contact forms, analytics, or a server that collects health information. The appointment checklist operates in your browser; the copy action uses your browser’s clipboard permission, and printing uses your browser’s print function. Your browser, hosting provider, or external websites may have their own privacy practices.

Before public launch

  • Have all medical content reviewed by a qualified hematology/oncology clinician.
  • Add a real review date and reviewer only after review has actually occurred.
  • Verify all links and country-specific services regularly.
  • If adding forms, analytics, accounts, or patient-submitted data, obtain appropriate privacy, security, consent, and legal review before collecting any health information.
Not a substitute for professional care

Online information can help you prepare questions, but only your clinical team can advise on your personal care.
